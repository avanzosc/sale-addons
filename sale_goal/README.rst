=========
Sale Goal
=========

This module extends Odoo Sales to define and track monthly and yearly sales goals for customers and salespersons.

It allows sales goals to be configured directly on customers and automatically aggregates those goals at salesperson level based on the customers assigned to each user. The module also tracks current monthly and yearly sales amounts from confirmed sales orders and provides goal achievement percentages for sales analysis.

Features
--------

- Defines monthly and yearly sales goals for customers.
- Automatically calculates the yearly sales goal from the monthly goal and vice versa.
- Aggregates customer sales goals at salesperson level.
- Tracks current monthly sales amounts for customers and salespersons.
- Tracks current yearly sales amounts for customers and salespersons.
- Automatically updates current sales amounts through scheduled actions.
- Excludes quotations, sent quotations, and cancelled sales orders from sales calculations.
- Provides access from salespersons to their assigned customers.
- Computes monthly and yearly goal percentages on sales order lines.
- Adds sales goal percentage measures to the Sales Analysis report.
- Provides customer filters to identify customers with or without defined sales goals.

Data Model
----------

**res.partner**

- Monthly Sales Goal (`sales_goal_monthly`):
  - Defines the monthly sales target for the customer.

- Yearly Sales Goal (`sales_goal_yearly`):
  - Defines the yearly sales target for the customer.
  - Automatically synchronized with the monthly goal using a 12-month period.

- Current Month Sale Amount (`current_month_sale_amount`):
  - Total sales amount generated by the customer during the current month.

- Current Year Sale Amount (`current_year_sale_amount`):
  - Total sales amount generated by the customer during the current year.


**res.users**

- Customers (`sale_partner_ids`):
  - Customers assigned to the salesperson through `res.partner.user_id`.

- Customer Count (`sale_partner_count`):
  - Number of customers assigned to the salesperson.

- Sales (`commercial_sale_order_ids`):
  - Sales orders assigned to the salesperson through `sale.order.user_id`.

- Monthly Sales Goal (`sales_goal_monthly`):
  - Sum of the monthly sales goals of all customers assigned to the salesperson.

- Yearly Sales Goal (`sales_goal_yearly`):
  - Sum of the yearly sales goals of all customers assigned to the salesperson.

- Current Month Sale Amount (`current_month_sale_amount`):
  - Total amount of valid sales orders assigned to the salesperson during the current month.

- Current Year Sale Amount (`current_year_sale_amount`):
  - Total amount of valid sales orders assigned to the salesperson during the current year.


**sale.order.line**

- Monthly Goal Percentage (`sales_goal_monthly_percentage`):
  - Percentage represented by the sales order line subtotal against the customer's monthly sales goal.

- Yearly Goal Percentage (`sales_goal_yearly_percentage`):
  - Percentage represented by the sales order line subtotal against the customer's yearly sales goal.


**sale.report**

- Monthly Goal Percentage:
  - Makes the monthly goal percentage available in the standard Sales Analysis report.

- Yearly Goal Percentage:
  - Makes the yearly goal percentage available in the standard Sales Analysis report.

Logic Overview
--------------

- Sales goals are configured on customers (`res.partner`).

- Monthly and yearly customer goals are synchronized:
  - Monthly goal × 12 → Yearly goal.
  - Yearly goal / 12 → Monthly goal.

- Salesperson goals are calculated from their assigned customers:
  - Monthly salesperson goal = Sum of customer monthly goals.
  - Yearly salesperson goal = Sum of customer yearly goals.

- Current sales amounts are calculated from sales orders:
  - Orders in `draft`, `sent`, or `cancel` state are excluded.
  - Sales amounts are based on the sum of `sale.order.line.price_subtotal`.
  - Monthly amounts include sales from the current month.
  - Yearly amounts include sales from the current year.

- Scheduled actions periodically recalculate:
  - Customer current monthly and yearly sales amounts.
  - Salesperson current monthly and yearly sales amounts.
  - Records without sales in the corresponding period are reset to zero.

- Goal percentages are calculated per sales order line:
  - Monthly percentage = `price_subtotal × 100 / customer monthly goal`.
  - Yearly percentage = `price_subtotal × 100 / customer yearly goal`.
  - If the corresponding goal is zero, the percentage is set to zero.

- Sales goal percentages are exposed in the standard Sales Analysis (`sale.report`) for reporting and analytical purposes.
